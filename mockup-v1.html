Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you scout.

How do I find the right property?
Start with the search card above: pick a category, set your town and price range, then refine with size and feature filters. Create a free account to save favorites and compare them side by side.
What is the process of buying a property?
Shortlist, inquire, make an offer, then our team walks you through document verification (TCT/CCT, tax declarations, and for farm land the DAR clearance) before any reservation fee, all the way to turnover.
How are listings verified?
Titles are checked at the Registry of Deeds and boundaries walked on-site before any listing goes live.
