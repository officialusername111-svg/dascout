Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you scout.

How do I find the right property?
Start with the search card above: pick a category (residential lot, farm land, commercial lot, or a building), set your town and price range, then refine with size and feature filters. Save favorites to compare them side by side.
What is the process of buying a property?
Shortlist, join a site tripping, make an offer, then verify documents (TCT/CCT, tax declarations, and for farm land the DAR clearance) before paying any reservation fee. Our licensed broker partners guide you through each step to turnover.
Can I schedule a site tripping online?
Yes. Every listing with open slots appears in the tripping schedule. Pick a date, tap "Book a slot", and you'll get a confirmation with the host broker's details.
How do I list my own property?
Register a free owner or broker account, submit your listing with photos and ownership documents, and it goes live once verification clears (usually within two business days).
